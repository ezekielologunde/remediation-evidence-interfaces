# Evidence-age interfaces for remediation approval

Status: Reserve. Historical candidate design, not a preregistration or proven novelty claim. Source: independent research portfolio dated 2026-10-04.

**Evidence-age interface study:** Compare a conventional recommendation card with a card exposing stale evidence, missing observations, contradictory sources and a defer option. Measure incorrect approval of deliberately invalid recommendations, correct approval, decision time and workload. Use a randomized, counterbalanced design with practitioner expertise modeled explicitly. [Human-Centered Explainability in AI-Enhanced UI Security Interfaces](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.22653) already compares explanation styles; only its abstract was inspected here. The candidate distinction is stale evidence during operational change approval. Participant availability, ethics review and a deeper HCI comparison are gates before starting. This is not currently a verified gap.
